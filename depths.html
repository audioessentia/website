DEPTHS

4D SOUND INSTALLATION

A 4D spatial audio installation exploring consciousness through immersive sonic art. This innovative experience combines advanced spatial audio technology with Indigenous wisdom traditions, guiding participants through a transformative journey from descent into shadow to integration and transcendence.

The installation incorporates authentic Shipibo Icaros, binaural beat recordings, and environmental soundscapes to facilitate deep states of consciousness exploration.

Premiered in September 2025 at Lobe Studio Vancouver, DEPTHS represents a unique fusion of technology and spirituality—a sonic ceremony that challenges participants to confront their shadows and emerge transformed.

The next iteration of DEPTHS is currently under development, with the majority of research and creative work taking place in South India. This new phase promises to deepen the exploration of consciousness through expanded sonic landscapes and cross-cultural spiritual practices.
