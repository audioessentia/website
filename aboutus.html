About Us

Audio Essentia is a collective of multi-disciplinary, multi-ethnic, multi-generational artists based in Vancouver. DEPTHS is their current installation which seeks to maintain genuine and reciprocal partnership with Peru's Shipibo community through touring activities by implementing proper cultural protocols.

This project will be pioneering methodologies to replicate the installation for touring immersive audio-visual installations globally, while contributing to emerging field of consciousness-exploring art through documented community impact.

Audio Essential will advance spatial audio/video technology applications internationally as well as establish new standards for mobile consciousness-exploring experiences and enhancing understandings of traditional wisdom integration in contemporary art globally, paired with indigenous-centered models for ethical international touring that prioritize reciprocity, respect, and the love for media arts.
